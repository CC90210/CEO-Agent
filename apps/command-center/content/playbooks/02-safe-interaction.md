# How to Work With Solara

Audience: Client-facing

The simplest way to get strong results from Solara is to be direct.

## Good asks

These are strong examples:

- "Show me the leads that need action today."
- "Draft a text follow-up for Marcus."
- "What renewals are most urgent this week?"
- "Explain why you ranked these leads this way."
- "What is blocking this application?"

## Better inputs create better outputs

When you can, include context.

Examples:

- "Draft a short follow-up for a lead who asked us to call after 4pm."
- "Prioritize leads for same-day outbound."
- "Rewrite this message to sound warmer and more confident."

## What to review before acting

Always glance at:

- Names
- Phone numbers
- Timing
- Offer details
- Anything client-facing before it is sent

Solara moves fast. Your review keeps the tone and business judgment tight.

## Best practice

Use Solara like a high-output team member:

- Ask for a recommendation
- Read the reasoning
- Approve or correct
- Move on quickly

You do not need perfect prompts. Plain English is enough.

## What not to do

Avoid vague asks like:

- "Handle everything"
- "Blast everyone"
- "Just send it"

That language creates messy execution. Clear scope creates clean output.
