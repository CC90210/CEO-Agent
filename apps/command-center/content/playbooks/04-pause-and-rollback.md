# Pause and Correct

Audience: Client-facing

If something feels off, do not panic.

The right move is usually:

1. Pause.
2. Confirm what changed.
3. Correct the direction.
4. Resume.

## When to pause

Pause and review when:

- A message looks wrong
- A lead is being prioritized badly
- A renewal recommendation does not match your real-world context
- A connection drops and Solara cannot complete the job cleanly

## What "pause" means in practice

Pause does not mean you failed.

It means:

- Stop using the draft or recommendation
- Check the lead, renewal, or workflow in question
- Give Solara clearer direction or ask CC for help

## How to recover

Good recovery language:

- "Do not use that version. Rewrite it with this context."
- "That lead should not be first. Re-rank with renewal timing in mind."
- "Hold here. Explain what you are seeing before we continue."

Clear correction is better than frustration. Solara responds well to direct course correction.

## The goal

You are not trying to prove Solara is perfect.

You are building a fast, reliable rhythm where:

- The dashboard shows what matters
- Solara handles the heavy lifting
- Your team steps in when judgment matters

That is the whole system working properly.
