# When to Loop in CC

Audience: Client-facing

Most days, your team should be able to work directly with Solara.

There are a few moments when you should bring in CC.

## Loop in CC when:

- A connection that used to be live suddenly goes offline
- Solara is missing obvious business context
- A recommendation feels risky or clearly wrong
- A key lead or renewal is too important to trust to automation alone
- You want a second opinion on how to work a deal

## Loop in CC fast when:

- The dashboard looks out of sync with reality
- Messages are drafted with the wrong tone or wrong facts
- A connected system stops updating
- Something important feels stuck

## A simple rule

If the issue is small and clear, correct Solara and keep moving.

If the issue could affect revenue, timing, compliance, or client trust, loop in CC.

## What to say

Keep it simple:

- What happened
- Who it affects
- What you expected instead
- Whether the work is blocked right now

That is enough for a fast handoff.
