# Meet Solara

Audience: Client-facing

Solara is your digital employee inside Sun Biz Funding.

She helps your team with:

- New lead intake from JotForm
- Text follow-up through Text Torrent
- Application progress
- Offer tracking
- Funded-deal visibility
- Renewal follow-up

## What this Command Center is for

Think of this dashboard as Solara's desk.

This is where you:

- See what needs attention first
- Ask Solara questions in plain English
- Review leads, renewals, and next steps
- Confirm what is connected and ready

## What Solara should handle for you

Solara is meant to remove the slow, repetitive work that clogs a funding pipeline.

That includes:

- Organizing new leads
- Prioritizing who should be contacted first
- Drafting follow-up language
- Keeping an eye on renewals
- Surfacing what is blocked

## What you should still own

Solara is strong, but she is not meant to replace judgment.

Your team still owns:

- Final deal decisions
- Pricing judgment
- Exception handling
- Sensitive client conversations
- Any move that feels unusual or high-risk

## Your best daily rhythm

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Read the welcome notes and pulse checks.
3. Ask Solara what needs action today.
4. Work the leads and renewals she surfaces.
5. Correct her when context changes.

The more clearly your team works with her, the sharper she becomes.
