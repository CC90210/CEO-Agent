# Cold Email Strategy — OASIS AI Solutions

> Strategic, personalized outreach that provides value before asking for anything.
> Last updated: 2026-03-04

---

## Core Philosophy

**Never bombard. Always personalize. Lead with proof.**

Every email should feel like it was written by a real person who spent 5 minutes looking at their business — because it was. The audit/mock-up approach works because it demonstrates capability, not just claims.

---

## The 3-Touch Sequence

### Touch 1: The "Quick Audit" Email (Day 1)
**Goal:** Open a conversation by showing you already understand their business.

**Subject Line:** `[First Name], I built something for [Business Name]`

**Body Framework:**
```
Hey [First Name],

I'm Conaugh McKenna — I run OASIS AI Solutions here in Collingwood.

I spent a few minutes looking at [Business Name] and found [2-3 specific things]:

1. [Specific observation] — e.g., "Your Google Business listing has 47 reviews but no automated review request system"
2. [Specific observation] — e.g., "Your booking page requires 6 clicks — I can get that to 2"  
3. [Specific observation] — e.g., "You're manually posting to Instagram — that can run on autopilot"

I put together a quick mock-up showing what these automations would look like for [Business Name]. No cost, no commitment — just want to show you what's possible.

Worth a 15-minute call this week?

Conaugh McKenna
Founder, OASIS AI Solutions
oasisaisolutions@gmail.com
```

**Attachments:**
- Personalized 1-page audit (see Audit Template below)
- NO generic company brochure — that kills the personal feel

---

### Touch 2: The "Follow-Up with Proof" Email (Day 3-4)
**Goal:** Re-engage with social proof or a different angle.

**Subject Line:** `Re: [Original subject]` or `the [Business Name] automation I mentioned`

**Body Framework:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Quick follow-up on my note from [day]. 

Since then, I actually [did something relevant]:
- Built a similar system for [comparable business type]
- [Specific result: "Their booking confirmations went from manual to automatic — saved them 8 hours/week"]

I genuinely think [Business Name] could see similar results, especially with [specific seasonal/timing angle].

If you're interested, here's a quick meeting link — no scheduling needed, just hop on whenever works:
[Google Meet link: https://meet.google.com/oqd-xpoq-fgw]

Talk soon,
Conaugh
```

---

### Touch 3: The "Last Chance" Email (Day 7-10)
**Goal:** Create gentle urgency without being pushy.

**Subject Line:** `closing the loop on [Business Name]`

**Body Framework:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Last note from me — I know you're busy running [Business Name].

If the timing isn't right, no worries at all. But I wanted to leave you with this:

The average service business owner spends 12+ hours/week on admin tasks that AI can handle. That's 600+ hours a year.

If you ever want to explore what that looks like for [Business Name], I'm here:
→ oasisaisolutions@gmail.com
→ meet.google.com/oqd-xpoq-fgw

Wishing you a great [season/week],
Conaugh McKenna
OASIS AI Solutions
```

## Personalized Audit Template

For each lead, create a 1-page audit covering:

### Section 1: Business Snapshot
- Business name, category, location
- Online presence score (website, Google Business, social media)
- Current pain points (inferred from their setup)

### Section 2: 3 Automation Opportunities (Specific to Their Business)

**For HVAC/Plumbing/Contractors:**
1. Automated quote follow-ups (customer submits form → instant email + SMS with estimate timeline)
2. Review collection engine (job completed → automated review request to Google/Yelp)
3. Seasonal campaign automation (heating season reminders, AC prep emails)

**For Wellness/Spas/Chiropractors:**
1. Appointment reminder system (reduce no-shows by 40%)
2. New client onboarding automation (intake forms → calendar → welcome email)
3. Social media content scheduling (before/after posts, testimonials)

**For Real Estate:**
1. Lead qualification chatbot (website visitor → qualified lead → CRM)
2. Listing alert automation (new listings auto-posted to social, emailed to buyer list)
3. Transaction coordination (offer accepted → automated checklist + timeline)

### Section 3: Estimated Impact
- Hours saved per week: [X]
- Estimated monthly value: [$ amount based on their hourly rate]
- Implementation timeline: [1-2 weeks typical]

## What NOT to Include

- ❌ Generic company brochure PDFs
- ❌ Long feature lists
- ❌ Pricing in the first email (discovery call first)
- ❌ Technical jargon (API, webhook, n8n, etc.)
- ❌ Multiple attachments — max 1 per email
- ❌ Any "limited time offer" language
- ❌ Any mention of AI "replacing" anyone

## Tracking & Metrics

For each cold email batch, track in LEAD_TRACKER.csv:
- Date sent
- Lead name / business
- Subject line used
- Open rate (if available)
- Reply received (Y/N)
- Meeting booked (Y/N)
- Outcome

**Target Metrics:**
- Open rate: 40%+
- Reply rate: 15%+
- Meeting rate: 5%+ of total sent
