# Canva

## Site

- URL patterns: `https://www.canva.com/`
- Auth assumptions: CC or Maven may be logged in locally.
- Agent owner: Maven
- Last verified: 2026-04-22

## Use Cases

- Read-only: inspect designs, templates, brand kit, exports.
- Draft-only: prepare layout notes.
- Approval required: publish, share externally, delete, overwrite brand kit, export private client assets.

## Traps

- Canvas content is often not DOM-readable.
- Use screenshots and exported files for verification.
- Account/workspace context matters.

## Approval Gates

Approval required before sharing, publishing, deleting, exporting sensitive client work, changing brand kit, or inviting users.
