# OASIS AI Solutions — What It Is and How to Position It

## What OASIS AI Is

OASIS AI Solutions is an AI automation agency founded by Conaugh McKenna (CC) in Collingwood,
Ontario, Canada. It sells monthly retainer engagements to local service businesses, deploying
custom AI automations that multiply client output without adding headcount.

The core value proposition: AI is the ultimate Swiss Army Knife for business growth. OASIS AI
eliminates the fear around AI adoption by educating clients and deploying systems that produce
measurable revenue impact.

**Founder identity:** Conaugh McKenna. In professional outreach and proposals, always use
the full name "Conaugh McKenna" — never "CC" (that nickname is for DJ/entertainment contexts only).

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

**Primary verticals:**
- HVAC companies
- Wellness businesses (physio, chiro, massage, med spa)
- Local service businesses with repeat customer models
- Any business spending time on manual follow-up, booking, or content

**Ideal client signals:**
- Has a client base of 50+ active customers
- Currently losing leads through slow follow-up or no automation
- Owner doing things manually that could be systemized
- Monthly revenue of $20K–$200K (can afford $400–$1,000/mo retainer)
- Operates in a competitive local market where speed-to-lead matters

**Anti-ICP (do not pursue):**
- Pure e-commerce (different automation stack)
- SaaS companies (wrong scale)
- Clients who want to own and manage the tech themselves
- Anyone below $10K/mo revenue (can't afford meaningful retainer)

## Differentiation

**Against other AI agencies:** OASIS AI is run by a 22-year-old who is deeply technical and
genuinely builds and operates the systems — not a reseller of third-party tools with a markup.
CC has lived internationally (Osaka, Dublin, Oslo), which gives credibility and perspective that
local agency operators lack.

**Against DIY:** Business owners do not have the time or knowledge to configure n8n, Supabase,
Claude API, and Stripe themselves. OASIS AI collapses 3–6 months of learning into a deployed
system in 2–4 weeks.

**Against no automation:** Every week without automation is a week where leads fall through cracks,
follow-up doesn't happen, and revenue is left on the table.

## Pitch Framework (NEPQ-aligned)

The NEPQ (Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questioning) approach applies here:
1. Never sound salesy. Open with genuine curiosity about their problem.
2. Lead with pattern interrupts: "I'm not sure if this is even a fit for you, but..."
3. Ask about their current pain before pitching anything
4. Quantify the cost of inaction (leads lost, hours wasted, revenue missed)
5. Position AI automation as the answer to the pain they just described themselves
6. The close is a natural conclusion, not a pressure play
