# UNIVERSAL DISCOVERY PLAYBOOK: OASIS AI SOLUTIONS

> [!NOTE]
> This playbook is built on the **NEPQ (Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questions)** framework to disarm resistance and lead prospects to their own conclusions about their need for automation.

## Phase 1: Connecting (Building Trust & Context)
*Goal: Shift from "Sales Pitch" to "Business Consultation."*

1.  "Can you walk me through the 'origin story' of [Company Name]—what inspired you to start this?"
2.  "What does a 'perfect' week look like for you and the team right now?"
3.  "How are you currently balancing 'on-the-tools' work vs. the administrative side of the business?"

## Phase 2: Situation (Fact-Finding & Friction Mapping)
*Goal: Understand the current tech stack and identifies where data "leaks."*

4.  "Walk me through the lifecycle of a lead: someone clicks 'Get a Quote' on your site—then what happens?"
5.  "How do you currently handle inquiries that come in after hours or over the weekend?"
6.  "When a job is finished, what’s the process for getting paid and securing a review?"
7.  "Which software tools (CRM, Email, Sheets, Calendar) are you using to keep the lights on right now?"

## Phase 3: Problem Awareness (Uncovering the Pain)
*Goal: Get the prospect to articulate the cost of their current manual processes.*

8.  "When that manual follow-up process breaks down, what usually happens to that lead?"
9.  "How much time would you say you (or your admin staff) spend 'copy-pasting' info between different apps?"
10. "What’s the biggest 'bottleneck' preventing you from doubling your crew size tomorrow?"

## Phase 4: Solution Awareness (The Gap)
*Goal: Discovery what they've tried (and why it didn't solve the problem).*

11. "What have you tried in the past to speed up your response times or automate the paperwork?"
12. "If you had a 'digital employee' that could handle 80% of your admin work for $1/hr, what’s the first task you’d offload?"

## Phase 5: Consequence (The Cost of Inaction)
*Goal: Visualize the long-term impact of staying manual.*

13. "If the business stays as manual as it is right now, where does that leave you in 12 months?"
14. "What happens to your profit margins as you scale, if your admin overhead scales at the same rate?"

## Phase 6: Commitment (The Close)
*Goal: Secure a next step (Audit or Retainer) without pressure.*

15. "Based on what we've discussed, is reducing this manual friction actually a priority for you this quarter?"
16. "Would it be helpful if I mapped out exactly where you're leaking hours and showed you a roadmap to plug those holes?"

## Technical Discovery (For OASIS Internal Use)
*Ask these if the conversation gets technical or for the "Automation Audit."*

*   **Communication:** SMS vs. Email vs. Phone?
*   **Data Storage:** Where is the 'Source of Truth'? (Google Sheets, CRM, Notebook?)
*   **Triggers:** What specific event "starts" a process? (Form fill, Stripe payment, Gmail tag?)
*   **Handoffs:** How does info move from the Office -> Field Crew?
