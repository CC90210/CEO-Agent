## Module 1: Distribution Channels Ranked by ROI

A lead magnet sitting on a landing page with no traffic produces zero leads. Distribution is where most agency owners underinvest. These five channels are ranked by return on time and money for AI agencies.

### Channel 1: Organic Social (Free, Highest ROI)

Organic social is the highest-leverage starting channel because it costs nothing and reaches people already engaged with your content.

**LinkedIn — Best B2B channel for agency lead generation**

The most effective format: value post with a comment trigger.

```
Post structure:
- Hook: Call out the specific pain point
- Body: Deliver 3-5 genuine insights (not a pitch)
- CTA: "I made a free [lead magnet] for [niche] businesses.
  Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send it directly."
```

Example:
```
HVAC business owners: here are 5 tasks your team is doing manually
that AI could handle in 2026.

[List 5 genuine, specific examples]

I built a free AI Readiness Scorecard specifically for HVAC companies.
It takes 5 minutes and shows you exactly where your biggest
automation opportunities are.

Comment "READY" and I'll send it directly.
```

**Instagram and TikTok — Short-form for visual demos**

Best format for AI agencies: screen recordings of automations in action. Show the before (manual process) and after (automated process). End with: "Link in bio to get the free [lead magnet]."

**X (Twitter/Threads) — Thread format**

Post a thread that covers the topic your lead magnet addresses. Final tweet: "I turned this into a full [checklist/calculator/guide] — free at [link]."

⚡ **QUICK WIN:** Post once on LinkedIn with a comment trigger. Reply to every comment with the landing page link and a personal note. Done manually the first week, this alone can generate 20–40 qualified leads.

### Channel 2: ManyChat Automation (Free + Paid)

**ManyChat** is a chatbot platform for Instagram and Facebook that turns comment triggers into automated DM flows. It is the most powerful tool for scaling organic social lead generation without adding work.

**How it works:**

```
You post on Instagram:
"Comment AI AUDIT to get my free AI Readiness Scorecard"
    ↓
Follower comments "AI AUDIT"
    ↓
ManyChat detects the keyword automatically
    ↓
Sends a DM: "Hey [Name], here's the link to your free scorecard: [link]"
    ↓
Follower opts in on landing page
    ↓
Lead captured in your email system
```

**Why this matters:** Instagram's algorithm rewards posts with high comment counts. The comment trigger mechanic generates the engagement that pushes the post to more people — while simultaneously capturing leads from every commenter. It compounds.

**Setting up ManyChat keyword triggers:**

1. Create a ManyChat account at manychat.com (free plan covers this)
2. Connect your Instagram or Facebook account
3. Go to Automation → New Flow → Comment Growth Tool
4. Set the keyword (e.g., "AI AUDIT", "READY", "SCORECARD")
5. Write the DM message with your landing page link
6. Set the comment reply (public, on the post): "Sent you a DM!"
7. Activate

💡 **PRO TIP:** Use an uppercase single word as your keyword ("READY" not "send me the checklist"). Simpler keywords have dramatically higher trigger rates because followers remember and use them correctly.

💀 **COMMON MISTAKE:** Requiring followers to follow your account before triggering the DM. This friction kills participation rates. Send the resource freely — they'll follow after getting value.

### Channel 3: Paid Ads

Paid traffic gets your lead magnet in front of cold audiences who have never heard of you. It requires budget but produces the most predictable, scalable lead volume.

**Facebook and Instagram Lead Form Ads**

The fastest way to run paid lead generation: Facebook's native lead form ads capture name and email without sending people to an external landing page.

| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| Cost per lead (B2B services) | $15–50 |
| Minimum test budget | $10–20/day |
| Minimum test duration | 7–14 days before optimizing |
| Audience targeting | Interest-based (industry) + Lookalike (existing contacts) |

**LinkedIn Lead Gen Ads**

Higher cost per lead than Facebook, but the quality is significantly better for B2B. Prospects fill out a pre-populated form without leaving LinkedIn.

| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| Cost per lead (B2B) | $50–150 |
| Best for | Decision-maker titles (Operations Manager, CEO, Owner) |
| Minimum budget | $50/day |
| Best format | Single image + short copy + lead form |

🧠 **KEY TAKEAWAY:** Start with organic and ManyChat. Only move to paid ads once you have confirmed your lead magnet converts and your nurture sequence produces booked calls. Paying to fill a leaky funnel is expensive.

### Channel 4: Email Signature & Existing Touchpoints

Your warmest traffic is already in your inbox. Add your lead magnet to every existing communication.

| Touchpoint | Conversion Rate | How to Add |
|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| Email signature | 25–40% | Link with one-line benefit statement |
| Invoice / proposals | 30–45% | Footer: "Get our free [resource] → link" |
| Client onboarding emails | 20–35% | "While we get started, here's a resource you might find useful" |
| LinkedIn profile | 15–25% | Featured section + About section link |
| Zoom background | Passive | Add your landing page URL as watermark |

⚡ **QUICK WIN:** Update your email signature right now. Add: "[Your Name] | Free AI Readiness Scorecard for [Niche] → [link]". This single change adds persistent lead generation to every email you send.

### Channel 5: SEO and Blog CTAs

If you publish blog content or are working toward organic search traffic, embed your lead magnet as an inline CTA and exit-intent popup.

| Placement | Conversion Rate |
|-----------|----------------|
| Inline CTA within blog post | 3–8% |
| Exit-intent popup | 5–12% |
| Hello bar (top of site) | 1–3% |
| Sidebar widget | 0.5–1% |

Exit-intent popups trigger when the cursor moves toward the browser's close button. They recover a meaningful percentage of visitors who would otherwise leave without converting. Tools: OptinMonster, Sumo, or ConvertBox.

## Module 2: The 5-Email Nurture Sequence

Getting someone's email address is the beginning, not the result. The nurture sequence converts subscribers into booked discovery calls over 8 days.

**The goal of each email:**

```
Email 1: Deliver value + establish credibility
Email 2: Start a real conversation
Email 3: Add more value + demonstrate depth
Email 4: Social proof from real client outcomes
Email 5: Make the offer
```

### Email 1 — Immediate Delivery (Send Instantly)

**Subject:** Your free [lead magnet name] is here

**Content:**
- Deliver the asset (direct link or download button)
- One paragraph reinforcing why this is valuable
- Set expectations: "Over the next few days, I'll send you a few related resources that [niche] businesses are using to [outcome]."
- No pitch. No ask. Just deliver.

**Tone:** Warm, helpful, no sales pressure.

### Email 2 — Day 2: Start a Dialogue

**Subject:** Quick question

**Content:**
- Ask one specific question about their current situation
- Keep it under 100 words total
- The question positions you as curious and accessible, not a salesperson

**Example:**
```
Subject: Quick question

Hey [First Name],

Quick one — after going through the scorecard, what was the biggest
surprise for you?

Most [niche] business owners I work with find [common insight] —
but I'm always curious whether that matches what you're seeing too.

Hit reply and let me know. I read every response.

[Name]
```

**Why this works:** The reply rate on this email is your engagement signal. Anyone who replies is a warm lead. Respond personally and immediately.

### Email 3 — Day 4: Add More Value

**Subject:** 3 things that helped [niche] businesses get started

**Content:**
- Share 3 related resources (a blog post, a case study, a video, a tool)
- Each resource should be genuinely useful
- Keep framing in terms of their outcomes, not your services

This email establishes that you are a source of ongoing value, not someone who gave away one thing and then immediately asked for money.

### Email 4 — Day 6: The Case Study

**Subject:** How [client type] saved [specific outcome]

**Content:**
- Share a specific client result with real numbers
- Tell the story: what was the problem, what was implemented, what was the result
- Be specific: "saved 40 hours per month" beats "saved a lot of time"
- End with: "This is the kind of result we consistently achieve for [niche] businesses."

💡 **PRO TIP:** If you do not yet have client results, use a hypothetical based on industry benchmarks ("A typical HVAC company with 5 technicians loses approximately 8 hours per week to manual scheduling — here's what that looks like automated"). Be transparent that it is a benchmark, not a specific client.

### Email 5 — Day 8: The Offer

**Subject:** If you want help with this

**Content:**
- Acknowledge they have been in your world for a week
- Make a clear, frictionless offer
- Three service tiers with distinct price points
- One clear CTA: a link to book a discovery call

**Example structure:**
```
Subject: If you want help with this

Hey [First Name],

You've now seen the scorecard, a few resources, and a real example
of what's possible.

If you want to actually implement this for your business, here's
how I can help:

→ Quick-Start ($X): [One specific deliverable, 1-week turnaround]
→ Core System ($X/month): [Core retainer, what's included]
→ Full Build ($X): [Done-for-you complete implementation]

If any of those feel right, you can book a free 30-minute call here:
[booking link]

No pressure either way — but if you're ready to move, that's the link.

[Name]
```

💀 **COMMON MISTAKE:** Presenting too many options. Three tiers is the maximum. More than three causes decision paralysis and reduces conversions.

## Module 3: Nurture Sequence Rules

These rules apply to every email in the sequence.

| Rule | Why It Matters |
|------|---------------|
| **One CTA per email** | Two CTAs split attention and reduce action on both |
| **Plain text over designed** | B2B audiences respond better to plain emails — they feel personal, not promotional |
| **Segment by lead magnet** | Subscribers who downloaded an ROI calculator have different intent than those who got a prompt library |
| **Respect cadence** | 8-day sequence = enough touchpoints without feeling aggressive |
| **After the sequence: monthly newsletter** | Keep warm leads in your world until they are ready |

**Segmentation rule:**

If you have multiple lead magnets, tag subscribers by which asset they downloaded. This lets you reference the specific thing they engaged with in every follow-up email, which dramatically increases relevance and open rates.

**ConvertKit tag example:**
- Downloaded ROI Calculator → tag: `roi-calculator`
- Downloaded Readiness Scorecard → tag: `readiness-scorecard`
- Email 4 subject for `roi-calculator` segment: "How [Client] Cut Their Costs by 34% (Inspired by the Calculator You Downloaded)"

## Module 4: ManyChat Full Setup Walkthrough

**Step 1: Connect your Instagram account**
- manychat.com → Settings → Channels → Instagram
- Authorize ManyChat to manage your DMs and comments

**Step 2: Create your lead magnet flow**
- Automation → New Flow → Name it "[Lead Magnet Name] Delivery"
- Add Trigger: Instagram Comments → keyword = your trigger word
- Add Action: Send DM with landing page link
- Add Comment Reply (public): "Sent you a DM with the link!"

**Step 3: Test the flow**
- Post a test comment with your keyword on your own post
- Confirm you receive the DM
- Confirm the public reply appears

**Step 4: Connect to your email system**
- On your landing page form: connect to ConvertKit or Mailchimp
- When someone opts in, they enter your 5-email nurture sequence automatically
- ManyChat gets them to the page; your email system takes it from there

🧠 **KEY TAKEAWAY:** ManyChat captures intent in the moment. When someone comments on your post, they are actively engaged with your content. That is the highest-intent moment to deliver your lead magnet. Automating that delivery means you capture leads while you sleep, at the gym, and during client calls.

**Part 1 — ManyChat Setup (30 minutes)**

1. Create a ManyChat account at manychat.com
2. Connect your Instagram or Facebook page
3. Set up a Comment Growth Tool with your keyword trigger
4. Write your DM delivery message
5. Test the full flow with a test comment

**Part 2 — Write All 5 Emails (60 minutes)**

Using the templates above:

1. Email 1: Write your delivery email. Paste in your direct download link.
2. Email 2: Write your "quick question" email. Make the question specific to your niche.
3. Email 3: Find 3 genuinely useful resources in your niche. Write the email.
4. Email 4: Write your case study email. Use a real client result or a benchmark-based hypothetical.
5. Email 5: Write your offer email. Define your three tiers. Add your booking link.

**Part 3 — Set Up the Sequence in ConvertKit**

1. Create a new sequence in ConvertKit
2. Paste in all 5 emails with correct send delays (0 days, 2 days, 4 days, 6 days, 8 days)
3. Create an automation: Form opt-in → Start sequence
4. Test by submitting your own form and confirming all 5 emails are queued

**Part 4 — Update One Existing Touchpoint**

Update your email signature with a link to your lead magnet landing page. Use this format:

```
[Your Name] | [Agency Name]
→ Free [Lead Magnet Name] for [Niche] Businesses: [landing page URL]
```
