## Module 1: The Creation Framework

Most people take 4–8 hours to build a lead magnet. With the right workflow, you can cut that to under 2 hours without sacrificing quality. The framework has five phases, and the order matters.

### Phase 1: Pick ONE Specific Problem

Your lead magnet solves exactly one problem for exactly one audience. Not three problems. Not a broad topic. One problem.

**How to find it:**

| Method | What to Do |
|--------|-----------|
| Client conversations | What do your existing clients ask most often before signing? |
| Sales call objections | What concern comes up in every discovery call? |
| Niche forums | What questions get the most replies in your niche's Facebook Groups or Subreddits? |
| Competitor research | What do competing agencies offer as lead magnets? What are they missing? |

⚡ **QUICK WIN:** The single best source for lead magnet ideas is your last 10 sales conversations. If three prospects asked the same question, that question is your lead magnet.

### Phase 2: Choose the Simplest Format That Delivers the Outcome Fast

Match the format to what your audience will actually use within 24 hours of downloading.

| If the Outcome Is... | Best Format |
|---------------------|------------|
| "Know if I'm ready for AI" | Checklist or Scorecard (PDF or web form) |
| "See how much I could save" | Calculator (Google Sheet or web tool) |
| "Have ready-to-use prompts" | PDF prompt library or Notion page |
| "Understand AI step by step" | 5-day email mini-course |
| "See what automation looks like for my business" | Loom video audit or live chatbot demo |
| "Get a working workflow" | n8n JSON template or Zapier Zap |

💡 **PRO TIP:** PDFs feel substantial and are easy to deliver. Calculators feel interactive and justify decisions. Videos feel personal and build the fastest trust. Choose based on what your prospect needs to feel, not what is easiest for you to produce.

### Phase 3: The Three-Agent AI Workflow

This is the core production system. Three AI tools, each doing what it does best.

```
Claude          → Extraction & structure
ChatGPT         → Formatting & variations
Gemini          → Personalization & local context
```

**How to run it:**

**Step 1 — Claude (Extraction):**
```
Prompt: "You are an expert in AI automation for [niche].
Create a 25-point AI Readiness Checklist for [niche] businesses.
Organize it into 5 categories with 5 questions each.
Each question should identify a specific operational inefficiency.
Output in plain text, numbered."
```

**Step 2 — ChatGPT (Formatting):**
```
Prompt: "Take this checklist and format it as a professional PDF document.
Add a scoring system: 0-1 point per item.
Add interpretation: 0-10 = Early Stage, 11-18 = Growing, 19-25 = Ready to Scale.
Add a brief recommendation paragraph for each score range.
Format with clear headers and bold key terms."
```

**Step 3 — Gemini (Personalization):**
```
Prompt: "Review this checklist for [niche] businesses in [region/country].
Add any industry-specific regulations, common tools, or local market context that makes this more relevant.
Identify any gaps that a [niche] business owner would immediately notice."
```

🧠 **KEY TAKEAWAY:** Each AI has a different strength. Claude writes with depth and structure. ChatGPT excels at reformatting and producing multiple variations quickly. Gemini adds current web context and localization. Using all three takes 20 minutes and produces work that would take a human 3–4 hours.

### Phase 4: Design in Canva

Canva has 1,000+ free templates for every lead magnet format: checklists, guides, workbooks, reports, and eBooks.

**The 10-minute Canva workflow:**

1. Go to canva.com → Search for "checklist template" or "ebook template"
2. Filter by your preferred color scheme
3. Pick a clean, minimal template (busy designs hurt credibility)
4. Drop in your AI-generated content
5. Replace placeholder images with relevant icons or illustrations
6. Export as PDF

**Design rules for lead magnets:**

| Rule | Why It Matters |
|------|---------------|
| Use 2 fonts maximum | More fonts = amateur appearance |
| Brand colors on cover only | Keep content pages clean and readable |
| One callout box per page | Too many highlights = nothing is highlighted |
| White space is content | Dense pages look difficult to consume |
| Page numbers always | Makes the resource feel complete and substantial |

💀 **COMMON MISTAKE:** Spending 3 hours on design. A clean, functional PDF that delivers the right information beats a beautiful PDF with weak content every time. Spend 80% of your time on content, 20% on design.

### Phase 5: Human Review Is Non-Negotiable

AI handles steps 1–4. You write the recommendations.

This is not about distrust of AI. It is about differentiation. The AI can structure the content and format it beautifully. But the specific recommendations — "HVAC companies at this stage should start with automated appointment reminders before touching inventory management" — come from your experience. That specificity is why someone hires you instead of downloading a generic guide.

**What to review before publishing:**

- [ ] Is every data point accurate? (AI hallucinates statistics — verify every number)
- [ ] Does the recommendation reflect your actual agency approach?
- [ ] Would you be comfortable if a prospect showed this to a competitor?
- [ ] Is the niche language correct? (Industry terms, acronyms, common tools)
- [ ] Does it lead naturally toward your service offering?

## Module 2: Landing Page Anatomy That Converts

Your lead magnet is only as effective as the page delivering it. A great resource behind a weak landing page will underperform.

**Benchmark:** The median landing page converts at 6.6%. A well-built lead magnet landing page should convert at 10–40%, depending on traffic quality.

### The 8 Elements of a High-Converting Landing Page

**Element 1: Headline**

The headline is the single most important element on the page. It must answer "what's in it for me?" within 5 seconds.

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Free AI Checklist" | "Find Out Exactly Where Your HVAC Business Is Losing Money to Manual Processes" |
| "Download Our Guide" | "The 25-Point AI Readiness Scorecard That 200+ Service Businesses Used to Identify Their First Automation" |
| "AI for Your Business" | "See How Much Time and Money You're Leaving on the Table — Free Calculator for Dental Practices" |

**Element 2: Form Fields**

Name + email only. Every additional field reduces conversion.

| Fields Required | Conversion Rate Impact |
|----------------|----------------------|
| Email only | Baseline |
| Name + Email | -11% |
| Name + Email + Phone | -37% |
| Name + Email + Phone + Company | -52% |

Reducing from 4 fields to 2 can lift conversion by 120%. Qualify leads in the nurture sequence, not the form.

**Element 3: CTA Button**

The button text is the last piece of friction before a conversion. Make it action-oriented and outcome-specific.

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| Submit | Get My Free Scorecard |
| Download | Send Me the Calculator |
| Sign Up | Start My Free AI Audit |
| Click Here | Get Instant Access |

**Element 4: Social Proof**

Place testimonials near the CTA, not at the bottom of the page. The closer social proof is to the conversion point, the higher it lifts conversion.

What works best for AI agency lead magnets:
- Job title + company type ("Operations Manager at 12-location HVAC company")
- Specific outcome ("Identified 3 automation opportunities in under an hour")
- Short quotes (2–3 sentences max)

**Element 5: Remove Navigation**

This is the single easiest technical change that reliably lifts conversion.

Removing navigation from a landing page lifts conversion by **16–28%**. Every navigation link is an escape route. Remove them all. The only action on the page should be the form submission.

**Element 6: Video**

A short explainer video above the fold boosts conversion by up to 86%. The video does not need to be polished.

What to cover in 60–90 seconds:
1. Who this is for and what problem it solves
2. What they'll get (the specific resource)
3. What they need to do (enter email)

💡 **PRO TIP:** Loom videos work perfectly here. A 90-second Loom recorded on your laptop converts just as well as a produced video for most agency audiences.

**Element 7: Mobile-First Design**

73% of lead magnet downloads happen on smartphones. Design for mobile first, then confirm it still looks correct on desktop.

Mobile checklist:
- [ ] Headline readable at 375px width
- [ ] Form fields finger-tap friendly (minimum 44px height)
- [ ] CTA button full width on mobile
- [ ] Text size minimum 16px body copy
- [ ] Images don't break layout at small sizes

**Element 8: Page Speed**

Every additional second of load time reduces conversion by 7%.

Target: under 2 seconds. How to get there:
- Compress all images before uploading (use tinypng.com)
- Use a fast hosting platform (Carrd, Vercel, Webflow)
- Remove unnecessary scripts and tracking pixels
- Use system fonts where possible

## Module 4: Gamma.app — Polished PDFs in Minutes

Gamma.app converts plain text into professionally formatted PDFs, presentations, and web pages. It is the fastest path from AI-generated content to a shareable, visual document.

**The Gamma workflow:**

1. Go to gamma.app → New AI presentation
2. Paste in your AI-generated content (from the Three-Agent workflow)
3. Choose a template that matches your brand colors
4. Gamma generates a fully formatted, visual document
5. Export as PDF

**What Gamma does well:**
- Automatic visual hierarchy (headings, bullets, callout boxes)
- Built-in icon library
- Consistent spacing and typography
- Fast: 2–3 minutes from text to finished PDF

💡 **PRO TIP:** Use Gamma for your first version. Once you know the lead magnet converts, invest time in a custom Canva design that matches your brand precisely. Gamma gets you to market fast; Canva makes it yours.

## 🔥 CHALLENGE: Build and Launch

**Part 1 — Create Your Lead Magnet (60 minutes)**

Using the value proposition you wrote in Lesson 1:

1. Run the Three-Agent AI Workflow for your chosen lead magnet type
   - Claude: generate the core content and structure
   - ChatGPT: format it with scoring systems, tables, or variations
   - Gemini: add niche-specific context and review for gaps

2. Drop the content into Canva or Gamma.app
3. Apply one design pass (clean template, your brand colors, page numbers)
4. Export as PDF

**Part 2 — Build Your Landing Page (45 minutes)**

1. Open Carrd.co → Start a new site → Use a "lead" template
2. Add your headline (benefit-first, answers "what's in it for me?")
3. Add form: name + email only
4. Connect form to ConvertKit or your email provider
5. Write your CTA button text (action + outcome)
6. Remove all navigation links
7. Preview on mobile — fix anything that breaks
8. Publish

**Part 3 — Verify**

- [ ] Load the page on your phone. Is it readable and easy to fill out?
- [ ] Submit a test form. Did you receive the confirmation email?
- [ ] Time the page load. Is it under 3 seconds?
- [ ] Read the headline out loud. Does it clearly answer "what do I get and why does it matter"?
