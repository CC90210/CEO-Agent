## Quick Image Creation Tips

1. **Canva** — Free templates for course graphics. Search "online course" or "tech infographic"
2. **Screenshots** — Real tool screenshots > stock photos. Annotate with arrows/boxes
3. **Diagrams** — Use Excalidraw (free) or Miro for flowcharts and architecture diagrams
4. **Consistency** — Pick 2-3 brand colors and stick with them across all images
5. **Size** — Skool lesson images work best at 1200x675px (16:9) or 1200x800px
6. **Dark theme** — Matches the accelerator's tech/builder vibe
