## Philosophy

1. **Replicable over complex** — If a student can't do it in 30 minutes, simplify it
2. **Tools over theory** — Teach by doing, not lecturing
3. **Real business value** — Every lesson connects to making money or saving time
4. **Progressive unlocks** — Students earn access to advanced tools as they level up
5. **Stay current** — AI moves fast; curriculum updates monthly
