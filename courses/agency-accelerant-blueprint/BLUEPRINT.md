# PHASE 1: FOUNDATIONS

> Every great agency is built on solid principles. Skip this and you'll build on sand.

## 1.1 Operating Principles

These are the non-negotiable rules for how you run your agency:

### The 5 Accelerant Principles

**1. Value Before Volume**
Don't chase 100 clients. Deliver incredible results for 5. Word of mouth from 5 happy clients beats 1,000 cold DMs.

**2. Systems Over Hustle**
If you're doing something more than 3 times manually, automate it. Your time is your most expensive resource.

**3. Specificity Wins**
"I help businesses with AI" loses to "I help HVAC companies in Ontario automate their lead follow-up so they never miss a quote again." Be specific.

**4. Speed of Implementation**
The person who implements fastest wins. Don't spend 3 months perfecting your logo. Spend 3 days getting your first client.

**5. Revenue First, Everything Else Second**
Every decision should pass one test: "Does this get me closer to revenue?" If no, it waits.

## 1.2 North Star Metrics

Track these numbers weekly. They tell you if you're winning or losing.

| Metric | What It Measures | Target (Month 1-3) | Target (Month 6+) |

# PHASE 2: OFFER DESIGN

> Your offer is the single most important thing in your agency. A great offer sells itself. A bad offer can't be saved by great marketing.

## 2.1 The Value Equation

**Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time to Result x Effort Required)**

To increase value:
- **Increase** the dream outcome (bigger transformation)
- **Increase** perceived likelihood (proof, guarantees, testimonials)
- **Decrease** time to result (speed of delivery)
- **Decrease** effort required (you do the work, not them)

## 2.2 Offer Design Framework

### Step 1: Define the Dream Outcome
What does your client's life/business look like AFTER working with you?

**Example:** "Your business generates 50+ qualified leads per month on autopilot, with an AI system that follows up, qualifies, and books appointments — while you sleep."

Your Dream Outcome: _______________

### Step 2: Pricing Strategy

| Model | Best For | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **Project-Based** | One-time builds | $2,000-$10,000 for AI chatbot setup |
| **Monthly Retainer** | Ongoing management | $500-$3,000/month for automation maintenance |
| **Performance-Based** | Confident in results | $500 base + $50 per lead generated |
| **Hybrid** | Best of both | $3,000 setup + $1,000/month retainer |

**Pricing Rule:** Never charge less than $500/month. If you charge $200/month, you need 50 clients to hit $10K. At $2,000/month, you need 5. Which is more realistic?

### Step 3: Positioning & Unique Mechanism

Your **Unique Mechanism** is WHY your solution works differently than everyone else's.

**Bad:** "We use AI to help your business."
**Good:** "We deploy the A.C.E. System (Automate, Convert, Expand) — a 3-phase AI implementation that turns your existing customer data into a 24/7 lead generation machine."

# PHASE 6: CONVERSION & SALES

> Marketing gets attention. Sales gets money. You need both.

## 6.1 Sales Process Design

### The Accelerant Sales Pipeline

```
Lead → Qualification → Discovery Call → Proposal → Close → Onboarding
```

| Stage | Goal | Tool |
|-------|------|------|
| **Lead** | Get their attention | Cold outreach, content, referral |
| **Qualification** | Filter serious buyers | Application form, pre-call questionnaire |
| **Discovery Call** | Understand their problem | 30-min Zoom, framework below |
| **Proposal** | Present the solution | Custom proposal or offer stack |
| **Close** | Get commitment + payment | Stripe checkout, contract |
| **Onboarding** | Start delivering | Phase 7 system |

## 6.2 Discovery Call Framework

### The 4-Phase Call Structure (30 minutes)

**Phase 1: Rapport & Agenda (3 min)**
- Thank them for their time
- Set the agenda: "I'd love to learn about your business, understand where you're at, and if it makes sense, show you how we can help. Sound good?"

**Phase 2: Deep Diagnosis (15 min)**
Ask these questions IN ORDER:

1. "Tell me about your business — what do you do and who do you serve?"
2. "What does your current process look like for [lead gen / follow-up / client management]?"
3. "What's working well right now?"
4. "What's the biggest bottleneck or frustration?"
5. "How much time/money do you think that costs you per month?"
6. "Have you tried to fix this before? What happened?"
7. "If we could solve [their problem] in [timeframe], what would that be worth to you?"

**Phase 3: Present Solution (8 min)**
- "Based on what you've told me, here's exactly what I'd recommend..."
- Walk through your offer stack
- Share a relevant case study
- Address the objection before they raise it

**Phase 4: Close (4 min)**
- "Does this sound like something that would work for your business?"
- If yes: "Great, the investment is [price]. I can send you the link to get started right now."
- If objection: Use your objection library (Phase 3.5)

# PHASE 8: FULFILLMENT & DELIVERY

> This is where most agencies fail. They can sell but can't deliver. Don't be that agency.

## 8.1 Business Architecture

### The 3-Layer Model

```
Layer 1: YOU (Strategy, Sales, Client Relationships)
    ↓
Layer 2: SYSTEMS (Automations, SOPs, Templates)
    ↓
Layer 3: TEAM (Developers, VAs, Specialists)
```

**Month 1-3:** You do everything (Layer 1 + 2)
**Month 4-6:** You systematize (Layer 2 becomes robust)
**Month 6+:** You hire (Layer 3 comes in)

## 8.2 Systemizing Processes

### Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

For every repeatable task, create an SOP:

```
SOP: [Task Name]
Trigger: [What starts this task]
Steps:
1. [Specific action]
2. [Specific action]
3. [Specific action]
Expected Output: [What the result should look like]
Time Estimate: [How long it takes]
Owner: [Who does this]
```

### Priority SOPs to Create First:
1. Client onboarding (Phase 7)
2. AI chatbot deployment

## 9.1 Client Retention Strategies

| Letter | Strategy | Action |

| **U** | Upsell strategically | Only suggest additions that genuinely help |
| **L** | Listen actively | Ask for feedback, act on it |
| **T** | Transparent about issues | If something breaks, tell them first |
| **S** | Strategic partnership | Position yourself as a partner, not a vendor |
