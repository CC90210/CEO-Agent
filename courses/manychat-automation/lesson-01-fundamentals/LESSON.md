## Module 1: What Is ManyChat?

**ManyChat** is a chatbot automation platform built for social messaging channels. It lets you automate conversations in Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, SMS, and email — without writing code.

The pitch is simple: people ignore email. They don't ignore DMs.

ManyChat sits between your client's social presence and their leads. When someone comments on a post, replies to a story, or messages the account, ManyChat catches it and responds instantly — asking qualifying questions, delivering lead magnets, booking appointments, or routing the conversation to a human.

### Official Meta Business Partner

ManyChat is not a grey-area tool. It is an **Official Meta Business Partner** — which means:

- It operates within Meta's Terms of Service
- It has a direct relationship with Meta's API teams
- Clients using it are not at risk of account bans from the platform itself
- It is the safe, professional choice for agency deployments

This matters when pitching clients. You're not deploying a scraper or a growth hack. You're using a Meta-approved system.

## Module 3: The Channels

ManyChat currently supports six channels. Not all are equal in maturity or priority. Here is an honest breakdown:

### Instagram (Primary — Start Here)

Instagram is where ManyChat drives the most value for most clients. The two killer features:

**Comment-to-DM:** When someone comments a keyword on a post or reel, ManyChat automatically sends them a DM. This turns every post into a lead generation machine. If a client posts a reel and says "Comment GUIDE to get our free checklist," every person who comments gets an instant personalized DM. The comment also boosts the post algorithmically (more engagement = more reach).

**Story Reply:** When someone replies to a story, ManyChat can catch that and trigger a flow. Highly personal — the person already engaged voluntarily.

Requirements: The client must have an **Instagram Business account** (not Creator) linked to a **Facebook Page**.

### Facebook Messenger

The original ManyChat channel. Mature, reliable, full-featured. Messenger bots are useful for clients with active Facebook Pages — local businesses, events, service providers. The audience skews older than Instagram. Same flow builder applies.

### WhatsApp

**WhatsApp Business API** integration. This is the highest-converting channel in markets where WhatsApp is dominant (UK, Canada, Latin America, Europe, India, Southeast Asia). Requires a WhatsApp Business account and Meta approval for message templates.

⚠️ **WARNING:** WhatsApp has strict template approval rules. Marketing messages must be pre-approved by Meta. Unapproved messages can get the number banned. Always use the template approval process for outbound messaging.

### TikTok (Beta)

TikTok comment-to-DM is available in beta. The mechanism is the same as Instagram — comment keyword on a TikTok, get an automated DM. Currently in limited rollout. Watch this channel — it will be significant when it fully launches.

### SMS

US and Canada phone number provisioning via ManyChat. Text message automation. High open rates (similar to DMs). Best for time-sensitive flows: appointment reminders, flash sale alerts. Requires opt-in compliance (more on this in Lesson 4).

### Email

Basic email automation built into ManyChat. Useful for multi-channel sequences where you collect an email in a DM flow and want to follow up there. Not a replacement for a dedicated email platform (Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign) — think of it as a backup channel in the same flow.

### Channel Priority for Agency Deployments

| Priority | Channel | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| 1 | Instagram | Largest audience, highest ROI on reels/posts |
| 2 | Facebook Messenger | Established user base, reliable |
| 3 | WhatsApp | Dominant in non-US markets |
| 4 | SMS | Highest urgency use cases |
| 5 | Email | Backup follow-up in multi-channel flows |
| 6 | TikTok | Beta — monitor but don't build client dependencies yet |

## Module 5: Account Setup Walkthrough

### Step 1: Create Your ManyChat Account

1. Go to **https://manychat.com**
2. Click "Get Started Free"
3. Sign up with your email or Google account
4. Select your use case (choose "Marketing Agency" or the closest option)

### Step 2: Connect a Facebook Page

ManyChat requires a Facebook Page as the foundation — even for Instagram. This is a Meta requirement.

1. From the ManyChat dashboard, click **"Connect Facebook Page"**
2. Log in with your Facebook account (the one that administers the Page)
3. Grant all requested permissions — do not skip any
4. Select the Page you want to connect
5. Confirm

If the client is the Page admin, they need to grant you Business Manager access first, then you connect their Page to your ManyChat account (or to an account you create under their name).

### Step 3: Connect Instagram Business Account

The Instagram account must be:
- Switched to a **Business account** (not Personal or Creator)
- Linked to the Facebook Page you just connected

To connect:
1. Go to **Settings → Instagram** in ManyChat
2. Click "Connect Instagram Account"
3. Select the Instagram Business account linked to your Page
4. Grant all permissions (message access, comment access)
5. Confirm

💀 **COMMON MISTAKE:** Trying to connect a Creator account or a personal account. ManyChat only works with Instagram Business accounts. If a client's account is set to Creator, they need to switch to Business in Instagram Settings → Account → Switch Account Type before you can connect.

### Step 4: Send Yourself a Test Message

Before building anything for a client, verify the connection works:

1. Go to **Live Chat** in the ManyChat sidebar
2. Click "New Conversation" → choose Instagram or Messenger
3. Search for your own account
4. Send a test message
5. Verify it appears in the ManyChat inbox

If the message doesn't appear, the permissions weren't granted correctly. Disconnect and reconnect, making sure to accept every permission screen fully.

### Step 5: Explore the Dashboard

Key areas to orient yourself:

| Section | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Dashboard** | Account overview, subscriber count, recent activity |
| **Flows** | Where you build automations |
| **Growth Tools** | Set up comment triggers, landing pages, widgets |
| **Live Chat** | Human inbox — see all conversations, jump in manually |
| **Contacts** | Subscriber list, tags, custom fields |
| **Broadcasts** | One-time or scheduled messages to your list |
| **Analytics** | Flow performance, open rates, click rates |
| **Settings** | Account connections, billing, integrations |

## Module 6: Why Agencies Need This in Their Stack

If you're building an AI automation agency, ManyChat is not optional — it's a revenue multiplier. Here is why:

**The deliverable is visual and immediate.** Unlike backend automations that clients can't see, a ManyChat flow produces instant, visible results. A client watches someone comment on their post and sees the DM arrive. That is a wow moment. Wow moments close deals and retain clients.

**It solves a real, expensive problem.** For most local businesses and e-commerce brands, leads from Instagram rot because no one follows up fast enough. The average response time for a business DM is over 10 hours. ManyChat responds in seconds. The ROI is measurable and undeniable.

**Recurring revenue is built in.** ManyChat requires ongoing management — new flows for campaigns, optimization of existing flows, new channel connections. That is monthly retainer work by design.

**The skill is learnable in a week.** You don't need to code. The flow builder is visual. A skilled agency operator can learn ManyChat well enough to deploy client flows within 5-7 hours of hands-on practice. The leverage is high.

🧠 **KEY TAKEAWAY:** ManyChat is the highest-ROI tool in the agency stack for client-visible results. It converts better than email, it's Meta-approved, it's visual, and it sells itself once a client sees it work.
