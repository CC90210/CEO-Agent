# OASIS AI — Revenue Daily Playbook

> **Goal:** $10,000 USD Net MRR by September 30, 2026.
> **Current:** ~$6,000/mo (BreezeAdvance). Gap: ~$4,000/mo = **8–10 new Starter/Growth retainer clients.**
> **Time left:** ~12 days. This is a war-time schedule.
>
> This is not a strategy doc. This is a checklist you execute every single day. Skip nothing.

---

## The Math That Makes Failure Impossible

| Revenue needed | # of clients at $500/mo | # of discovery calls needed (25% close rate) | # of conversations needed (50% book rate) | # of daily touches to get there |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $4,000/mo | 8 clients | 32 calls | 64 conversations | **5–8 new conversations/day** |

You don't need to be good at sales. You need to **start enough conversations** that the math works in your favor. 64 conversations across 12 days = ~5 per day. That's the only number that matters.

## ☀️ MORNING BLOCK (7:00 AM – 9:00 AM) — Pipeline Loading

These tasks put leads INTO your pipeline. Without them, everything downstream starves.

### Task 1: Content Post (30 min) — Non-Negotiable

> **Why:** Content is your 24/7 salesperson. Every post is a lottery ticket for inbound leads.

- [ ] **Post 1 founder-journey piece** to Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Threads, TikTok
  - **Topic rotation (pick one daily):**
    1. "Here's what I automated for a client this week" (show the before/after)
    2. "I saved [client] X hours/week — here's how" (screen recording, 60 sec)
    3. "Most businesses lose money because of [specific problem]. Here's the fix." (educational)
    4. "Day in my life building an AI automation agency at 24" (documentary)
    5. "Here's exactly what our $500/mo retainer includes" (transparency play)
  - **Format:** Short video (< 90 sec) OR carousel (4 slides max)
  - **Hook must be in the first 3 seconds / first line.** No "Hey guys!" No "In this post..."
  - **CTA:** "DM me 'AI' if you want to see how this works for your business"

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do NOT skip this. A day without a post is a day without a chance at inbound. Your GPU box can render videos. Your content bible has the pillars. Just post.

### Task 2: Identify 10 Prospects (30 min) — Non-Negotiable

> **Why:** You need names to reach out to. No names = no conversations = no money.

- [ ] **Find 10 local service businesses** that fit your ICP:
  - **ICP:** Service businesses doing $20K–$500K/mo revenue, 2–50 employees, missing leads because they don't follow up fast enough
  - **Industries that convert:** HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, roofing, dental, real estate, MCA/lending, fitness studios, med spas, law firms
  - **Where to find them:**
    - Google Maps: search "[industry] near [Montreal / any city]" — look for ones with 50+ reviews but no automation visible on their site
    - Instagram: search local hashtags (#montrealbusiness #smallbusinessowner) — find ones posting but clearly not automated
    - LinkedIn: search "Owner" or "Founder" + industry + location
    - Facebook groups: local business owner groups (lurk, don't pitch in the group)
  - **What to capture per prospect:** Name, business name, phone, email, Instagram handle, one specific thing you noticed about their business that you could improve

### Task 3: Send 10 Outreach Messages (30 min) — Non-Negotiable

> **Why:** This is the engine. 10 messages/day × 12 days = 120 touches. At 5% reply rate = 6 conversations = 2-3 calls = 1 client minimum.

- [ ] **Send 10 personalized DMs or emails** using the NEPQ pattern interrupt:

**Instagram DM template (customize every time):**
```
Hey [Name] — saw your [specific post / review / thing you noticed]. 
Quick question — how are you handling [lead follow-up / booking / 
customer communication] right now? I work with [industry] businesses 
on automating that stuff and was curious if it's something you've 
ever looked into.
```

**Email template (use send_gateway):**
```
Subject: Quick question about [their business name]

Hey [Name],

I was looking at [their business] and noticed [specific observation — 
e.g., "you've got great reviews but your website doesn't have a way 
to capture leads after hours"].

I'm curious — how are you currently handling [their specific pain 
point]?

I help [industry] businesses automate [specific thing] so they don't 
lose leads. Not sure if it'd be a fit, but happy to show you what 
it looks like if you're open to it.

— Conaugh
OASIS AI Solutions
```

> [!WARNING]
> **Rules:**
> - NEVER copy-paste the same message to multiple people. Personalize every single one.
> - NEVER lead with a pitch. Lead with curiosity.
> - NEVER mention price in the first message.
> - Reference something SPECIFIC about their business. "I saw your website" is not specific. "I noticed your Google reviews mention long wait times for callbacks" IS specific.

## 🔥 MIDDAY BLOCK (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM) — Conversion Engine

These tasks turn conversations into money.

### Task 4: Follow Up on Every Open Conversation (30 min) — Non-Negotiable

> **Why:** 80% of sales happen between the 5th and 12th follow-up. Most people quit after 1.

- [ ] **Check every DM and email thread** from the past 7 days
- [ ] **Reply to every message** within the same day — speed = trust
- [ ] **Escalation cadence for non-responders:**
  - Day 1: Initial message (morning block)
  - Day 3: "Hey [Name] — just bumping this up. No pressure, just curious if [question from original message]?"
  - Day 7: "Last one from me — if this isn't the right time, totally get it. But if you ever want to explore automating [thing], the offer stands."
  - Then stop. Don't burn the bridge.

### Task 5: Book Discovery Calls (15 min)

> **Why:** A conversation in DMs is worth $0. A call is worth $500/mo+.

- [ ] For every prospect who engages (replies with interest), **immediately offer a 15-min call:**
  - "This is easier to show than explain — want to hop on a quick 15-min call this week? I can show you exactly what it'd look like for [their business]. No pitch, just a walkthrough."
  - Send your Calendly / booking link
  - **Goal:** Book 2-3 calls per week minimum

### Task 6: Run Discovery Calls (as scheduled)

> **Why:** This is where money happens.

- [ ] Follow the NEPQ framework (already in your [[knowledge/wiki/client-playbook]]):
  1. Open: "I'm not sure if this is even a fit — can I ask you a few questions first?"
  2. Situation: "Walk me through how you currently handle [their problem]"
  3. Problem: "What happens when [consequence of their problem]?"
  4. Implication: "How much business do you think you're losing from that?"
  5. Need-payoff: "If you could fix that and never think about it again — what would that be worth?"
  6. Close: "Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd build for you: [scope]. It's $500/mo. Want to get started this week?"

> [!TIP]
> **After every call**, within 60 seconds:
> - Log the call in the CRM
> - Send a follow-up message: "Great talking — here's what we discussed: [2 bullets]. I'll send the proposal today."
> - Generate the proposal: `python scripts/proposal_generator.py generate`

## 🌙 EVENING BLOCK (7:00 PM – 8:30 PM) — Leverage & Compound

These tasks create assets that work while you sleep.

### Task 7: Create One Piece of Proof (30 min) — Non-Negotiable

> **Why:** Social proof closes more deals than any pitch ever will. Every piece of proof is permanent ammunition.

- [ ] **Pick one and do it today:**
  - [ ] Record a 60-second Loom of a real automation running (blur client data)
  - [ ] Screenshot a result (leads captured, time saved, cost reduced) and turn it into a branded graphic
  - [ ] Write a 3-paragraph case study: "Client X had [problem]. We built [solution]. Now they [result]."
  - [ ] Record yourself walking through the Command Center dashboard — show it's REAL software, not vaporware
  - [ ] Ask SunBiz or BreezeAdvance for a testimonial quote — even 1 line is gold
  - [ ] Film a before/after: "Here's what [process] looked like before → here's what it looks like automated"

### Task 8: Engage on 20 Posts (15 min)

> **Why:** Instagram and LinkedIn reward engagement. Commenting on prospects' posts = warm intros without cold messaging.

- [ ] Find 20 posts from:
  - Your prospects (the 10 you identified this morning)
  - Local business owners in your city
  - Other agency owners (networking, not competition)
- [ ] Leave a **real, thoughtful comment** — not "Great post! 🔥" but an actual insight or question
- [ ] This is a long game. In 2 weeks, when you DM them, they already recognize your name.

### Task 9: Second Content Post (20 min)

> **Why:** Two posts/day = 2x the surface area for inbound.

- [ ] Post one educational / proof-of-work piece:
  - Screen recording of you building something
  - "Today I automated [X] — here's what happened"
  - Carousel: "5 things every [industry] business should automate"
  - Repost a morning piece to a different platform with adapted copy

## 📊 WEEKLY NON-NEGOTIABLES (Pick a day — Sunday works)

### Task 10: Pipeline Review (30 min)

- [ ] Open your CRM and categorize every active lead:
  - **Hot** (replied, interested, call booked or pending) → Follow up TODAY
  - **Warm** (replied but non-committal) → Send value-add content, re-engage
  - **Cold** (no reply after 3 touches) → Archive, move on
- [ ] Count: How many conversations happened this week? How many calls booked? How many proposals sent?
- [ ] If pipeline < 15 active conversations → **double outreach volume next week**

### Task 11: Case Study / Offer Refinement (30 min)

- [ ] Turn one real client result into a publishable case study
- [ ] Review your offer: Is "$500/mo for AI automation" landing? If not, test a different frame:
  - "We build your AI employee for $500/mo" (makes it tangible)
  - "$500/mo to never miss another lead" (outcome-focused)
  - "Pay less than a part-time hire, get more than a full-time team" (comparison)

### Task 12: Ask for Referrals (15 min)

- [ ] Message every current/past client:
  - "Hey [Name] — do you know any other business owners who'd benefit from what we built for you? Happy to give them a free audit."
- [ ] Message every connection who's said something positive about your work
- [ ] Offer a referral incentive: "If they sign up, your next month is free"
