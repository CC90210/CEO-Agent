# THE BENNETT STRATEGY: Technical Community Operations Plan
**Owner:** CC McKenna (Technical Community Manager)
**Client:** Bennett (AI Community Lead)
**Role:** Technical Expert, Community Manager, & Strategic Partner

## 1. THE VISION: "The Agentic Business Operating System" (BOS)
The primary value proposition is shifting from "just building bots" to building a **Unified Business Operating System.** 
We provide the **File Structure (Business-Empire-Agent)** and **Orchestration (MCP + CLI)** that allows any student to plug-and-play their own apps.

## 2. OPERATIONAL PILLARS

### A. Triage & Technical Support (The Efficiency Layer)
*   **The 3-Tier Support Model:**
    *   **Tier 1 (General Q&A):** Automated responses using our internal Knowledge Base (Memory-MCP) to handle 80% of routine questions.
    *   **Tier 2 (Architectural Help):** Direct assistance from CC on fixing Supabase/Stripe/Vercel integrations using the BOS templates.
    *   **Tier 3 (Bennett Escalation):** High-ticket members or fundamental business pivots are delegated to Bennett with a "Context Summary" (no more digging through chats).
*   **Live Call Strategy:**
    *   CC acts as the "Technical Grounding" on calls—translating Bennett's vision into immediate, actionable architectural steps for students.

### B. Reporting & Community Health (The Partnership Layer)
*   **Weekly Friction Report:** A 1-page summary for Bennett detailing:
    *   Top 3 technical bottlenecks students are facing.
    *   Community sentiment/vibe check.
    *   Opportunities for new content/modules based on common questions.
*   **Strategic Growth Ideas:** Periodic "Brain Dumps" from CC on new entrepreneurial angles, AI trends, and potential partnership expansions.

### C. Resource & Knowledge Management
*   **The "Agent-in-a-Box" Blueprint:** Providing students with the exact directory structure (`brain/`, `memory/`, `skills/`, `scripts/`) that makes their AI agents "self-aware" and efficient.
*   **Curated Repo Registry:** Maintaining an "Elite Repository List" (Awesome-MCP, Rulesync, etc.) so students always have the most cutting-edge tools.

## 3. TECHNICAL READINESS (The "CC Advantage")
*   **Extensible Architecture:** The Business-Empire-Agent is built to be **software-agnostic.** Whether a student uses Stripe, Lemonsqueezy, or Shopify, the integration layer (scripts/tool.py) remains the same.
*   **CLI-First Workflow:** Our custom CLI tools allow for faster debugging and deployment than any web UI, giving us (and the students) a significant speed advantage.
*   **Autonomous Documentation:** We use `read_file` and `grep_search` to automatically document community solutions, building a "Self-Healing FAQ."
