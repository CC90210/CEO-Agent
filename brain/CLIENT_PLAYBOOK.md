# OASIS WEBSITE SALES PLAYBOOK V4

## The Rep's Job

Find one visible website problem, connect it to lost trust/calls/bookings, qualify the owner, and book CC or Adon. Do not quote custom work, negotiate, promise feasibility, or turn the call into an AI lecture.

## Core Cold Call

> “Hey [Name], it’s [Rep] with OASIS. We haven’t spoken before—this is a cold call. I was looking at [Company] and noticed [specific website issue]. Can I take 30 seconds to explain why I called, then you can tell me if it’s irrelevant?”

> “When someone checks you out on their phone, [issue] makes it harder to [call/book/request a quote]. I’m curious—how much of your new business is supposed to come through the website today?”

Diagnose: What happens after a form or missed call? Who follows up? Is improving this already on the list? Are you the decision-maker? If the right fix starts at $2,000, is that a real conversation?

Position: “OASIS rebuilds the website around conversion first. If discovery exposes a follow-up leak, we can attach one focused automation. I don’t scope or price it on a cold call—CC or Adon will show you the audit and options.”

Book: “I can put you with [CC/Adon] on [option A] or [option B]. Which works?” Record the observed issue, qualification answers, selected founder, meeting time, and promised demo.

## Track Angles

- Trades: mobile click-to-call, emergency routing, service-area trust, quote follow-up.
- Professional services: authority, clear service paths, intake friction, document routing.
- Wellness/beauty: mobile booking, service presentation, reviews, no-show recovery.
- Home services: before/after proof, local SEO, estimate requests, missed-call recovery.

## Gatekeeper, Voicemail, Follow-Up

Gatekeeper: “I’m calling about a specific issue on the company website that may be costing inquiries. Who owns the website and new-customer flow?”

Voicemail: “Hi [Name], [Rep] from OASIS. I noticed [one concrete issue] on [Company]’s website that affects [calls/bookings]. I’ll send a short note so you can see exactly what I mean. My number is [number].”

Follow-up references the same observed issue and asks for a 15-minute audit walkthrough. Never send a generic capability dump.

## Rep Pipeline — Button by Button

1. Assigned: read the URL, audit finding, ICP track, contact details, and assigned-rep name. Make the first call.
2. Attempting Contact: choose No answer or Voicemail left and set the next follow-up time. Every open lead must have a next action.
3. Connected: ask the discovery questions and either mark Lost or complete all four qualification checks.
4. Qualified: open the OASIS Google Meet calendar, choose CC or Adon, confirm the meeting time, and write the promised demo angle in plain language.
5. Founder Meeting: verify the handoff is saved. Do not change price, scope, delivery timing, or automation feasibility. The founder now owns the deal.

The rep's work is complete when the founder handoff is accurate, not when the client pays. Attribution remains with the Agent assigned when the founder meeting was booked.

## Objections

- Send information: “I will. Should I focus on the mobile issue, lead capture, or both?” Then book.
- Existing provider: “Good. Is their priority maintenance or measurable calls and bookings? We can complement them if a conversion gap remains.”
- Too expensive: “I haven’t priced your scope. The starting point is $2,000; the founder recommends it only if the business case supports it.”
- Bad timing: “What needs to change for this to become timely, and when should we revisit?”
- Not interested: “Is the website already producing enough calls, or is it simply not a priority?” Exit after one diagnostic attempt.
- No website needed: “When referrals check you online before calling, what do they see? The website owns that trust step.”

## Founder Call — 30 Minutes

1. Reset (0–3): confirm the rep notes and desired outcome.
2. Diagnose (3–10): quantify trust, conversion, follow-up, and operational gaps.
3. Audit/demo (10–18): show current friction and a tailored direction.
4. Tier anchor (18–23): present Authority, Growth, then Essential; recommend one.
5. Automation discovery (23–26): prescribe only add-ons tied to an admitted leak.
6. Close (26–30): confirm scope, request the 50% deposit, and schedule onboarding.

Close: “Based on what you showed me, [package] is the right fit: [setup] and [monthly], with [automation]. We start with 50% today and the balance before launch. Is there anything stopping us from scheduling onboarding?”

## Operations SOP

1. Adon/APEX researches and enriches domain, owner, contact data, territory, ICP, website condition, and audit evidence in the upstream `leadgen_*` system, then promotes deduplicated rows into `tenant_records`. The sales engine begins at that promotion boundary and does not run a competing scraper.
2. Assign controlled rep batches and a next action. Assignment sends nothing.
3. Booking freezes rep attribution and stores founder, time, qualification, audit findings, and promised demo.
4. Founder verifies the issue, selects a package hypothesis, and prepares one relevant automation demo.
5. Proposal is generated from structured package/add-on data; no unapproved custom promise.
6. Payment records collected values and creates exactly one commission accrual and onboarding record.
7. Onboarding collects brand, domain/DNS status, business details, services, locations, assets, copy, integrations, and approval contact.
8. Fulfillment runs discovery → assets → scaffold → build → responsive/accessibility/SEO/forms QA → review → DNS launch → analytics/backups/maintenance.
9. Scope change, custom integration, legal claim, credential gap, payment dispute, or missed launch date escalates to CC/Adon.

## Definition of Launched

Mobile/desktop layouts, keyboard navigation, metadata, local SEO basics, analytics, lead forms, notifications, links, performance, backups, DNS, and client approval must pass. Monthly maintenance begins on launch day.
