# Cold Outreach Email

## When to Use

First contact with a prospect who has never spoken to CC. This is not a pitch — it is a pattern interrupt designed to create curiosity and open a dialogue. The goal of this email is a reply, not a sale.

**Framework:** Jeremy Miner NEPQ — pattern interrupt, questions over statements, "I'm not sure if..." framing to eliminate sales resistance.

---

## Subject Line Options

Choose the one that fits the prospect's context most specifically. Specificity beats cleverness every time.

1. `Quick question about {{prospect_company}}'s [specific process]`
2. `I'm not sure if this is relevant to {{prospect_company}}...`
3. `Noticed something about {{industry}} businesses like yours`
4. `[Mutual connection] mentioned I should reach out`
5. `Your [specific thing you noticed on their site/LinkedIn] caught my attention`

**Rule:** Never use "I" in the subject line. Never mention your company name in the subject line. The subject exists to get the open, nothing else.

---

## Email Body — Variant A (Direct Pain Point)

> Use when you have identified a specific operational gap from their website, LinkedIn, or job postings.

Hi {{prospect_name}},

I'm not sure if this is relevant to {{prospect_company}} right now, but I've been working with a few {{industry}} businesses who were dealing with {{specific_pain_point}} — and it was quietly costing them more than they realized.

We helped one of them [specific outcome — e.g., "cut their lead response time from 3 days to 4 minutes"] using AI automation. {{social_proof_metric}}.

I'm curious — is {{specific_pain_point}} something that's on your radar, or is your current setup working well enough that it's not a priority?

No agenda here. Just figured it was worth a quick note.

Best,
Conaugh McKenna
Founder, OASIS AI Solutions
[Phone / Calendar link optional]

---

## Email Body — Variant B (Curiosity Hook)

> Use when you do not have specific pain point intel. Relies on industry-level insight to create relevance.

Hi {{prospect_name}},

I'm not sure if {{prospect_company}} runs into this, but most {{industry}} businesses I talk to are losing somewhere between [X hours/week] and [Y dollars/month] to [specific manual process they likely recognize] — and they usually don't realize it until they see what it looks like automated.

I've been helping businesses like yours fix exactly that. {{social_proof_metric}}.

Is that something you've been thinking about, or is it not really a problem for you at this stage?

Either answer is helpful — just want to make sure I'm not wasting your time with something that doesn't apply.

Best,
Conaugh McKenna
Founder, OASIS AI Solutions

---

## Email Body — Variant C (Referral / Warm Signal)

> Use when a mutual connection referred you, or the prospect engaged with your content.

Hi {{prospect_name}},

[Mutual contact / I noticed you commented on / You recently liked] — figured that was a reasonable excuse to reach out.

I work with {{industry}} businesses on AI automation — specifically helping them [specific outcome]. [Mutual contact] thought we might be a fit to talk.

I'm not going to pitch you anything on a cold email. I'd rather ask: is {{specific_pain_point}} something that's actually causing friction in your business right now, or is it more of a "nice to solve eventually" thing?

Worth a quick conversation or not worth your time at all?

Best,
Conaugh McKenna
OASIS AI Solutions

## Personalization Checklist

Before sending, verify:
- [ ] Subject line references something specific to their company (not generic)
- [ ] Body references their industry or a specific business type — not "businesses like yours" without context
- [ ] "I'm not sure if this applies" framing is present — this is non-negotiable (kills sales resistance)
- [ ] No product/service pitch in the first email — only questions
- [ ] Social proof metric is specific (a number, a percentage, a timeframe) — not vague
- [ ] CTA is a question, not "book a call" or "let me know if you're interested"
- [ ] Email is under 150 words (Variant A or B) or under 200 words (Variant C)
- [ ] Signature uses full name "Conaugh McKenna" — not "CC" for B2B outreach
