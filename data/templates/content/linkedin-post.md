## Formatting Rules (LinkedIn)

- **Max:** 3,000 characters
- **First line IS the hook** — if it doesn't stop the scroll, nothing else matters
- **Line breaks liberally** — mobile readability is everything
- **Use → or • for lists** (not numbered unless sequential steps)
- **End with a question or CTA** (drives comments, which drives reach)
- **No hashtags mid-text** — 3-5 at bottom max, or skip entirely
- **No links in body** — put links in first comment (LinkedIn penalizes posts with links)
- **Emojis:** Sparingly. One per section max. Never as decoration.
- **Voice:** Introspective, raw, honest. Write like talking to a friend at 2am.
