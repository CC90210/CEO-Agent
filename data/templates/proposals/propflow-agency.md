## What PropFlow Is

PropFlow is tenant-screening and landlord-automation software: application intake, screening workflows, and the repetitive communication landlords drown in — built by operators who run rentals, not a generic SaaS team.

OASIS AI Solutions owns and operates PropFlow (Conaugh McKenna + Adon, 50/50 partners).

## Partnership Models

### Model A — Referral Partner

You send landlords/property managers to PropFlow; we close, onboard, and support.

- **Your share:** XX% of collected first-year revenue on referred accounts
- Tracking via unique link/code; reported monthly; paid on collection

### Model B — White-Label

PropFlow runs under your brand for your landlord portfolio or client base.

- **License:** $X,XXX/month flat + per-door fee of $XX above YY doors

- You own the client relationship; we run the infrastructure silently

### Model C — Market Joint Venture

We launch PropFlow into a market you know well (region or niche); revenue split ZZ/ZZ after costs.
