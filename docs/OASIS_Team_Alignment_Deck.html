Objection Playbook
Handling Pushback

"Send me information"

"I will. Should I focus on the mobile issue, lead capture, or both?" → Then book.

"Too expensive"

"I haven't priced your scope. Starting at $2K — the founder recommends it only if the business case supports it."

"Bad timing"

"What needs to change for this to be timely, and when should we revisit?"

"Already have a provider"

"Is their priority maintenance or measurable calls and bookings? We complement them if a conversion gap remains."

The Cold Call
The Opening

"Hey [Name], it's [Rep] with OASIS. We haven't spoken before — this is a cold call. I was looking at [Company] and noticed [specific website issue]. Can I take 30 seconds to explain why I called, then you can tell me if it's irrelevant?"

  • Pattern interrupt — own the cold call upfront
  • Lead with their specific problem, not our product
  • Ask for permission, not attention
  • Diagnose before pitching — always
