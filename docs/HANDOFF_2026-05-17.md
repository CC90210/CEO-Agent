# Known gap: timeline + alerts surface only on lead entity today

The Timeline panel + missing-info banner mount on the lead detail page
only. Applications and offers don't have them yet, even though lender
threads (the most operationally important conversation surface for the
funding shop) happen at the application level. Generalizing requires
either:

  * pivot `/api/leads/[id]/timeline` to `/api/records/[entity]/[id]/timeline`
    (preferred — single endpoint, every entity gets it for free), OR
  * duplicate route + duplicate panel per entity (worse).

Either way it's a real piece of work (~2h) — not done in Round 3.5.
Listed below in the queued items.

The classifier writes to `lead.missing_info` not `application.missing_info`
deliberately — the lead is the long-lived record that survives multiple
applications. If we move the field to application later, the classifier
write path needs to update too.
